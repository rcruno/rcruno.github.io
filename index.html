My name is Ryan Runo; I am a student at Millersville University majoring in Tech Ed. I hope to one day become a Tech Ed teacher for a high school where I can not only enjoy teaching hands on work, I can also further pursue my favorite sport growing up by coaching football. I am a transfer student from Sussex County, New Jersey, and I hope to graduate in the spring of 2027. I enjoy multiple outdoor hobbies; my favorite being fishing.

This website is made to give insight to incoming students for the class; Communications and Information Systems (AENG110). The class is described as an overview of communication systems and technologies utilized by humans to design, compose, send, receive, and understand ideas and information. Emphasis is on graphic and electronic media. This website will display some of the projects I have completed throughout the entire semester while also giving a brief description of each.
